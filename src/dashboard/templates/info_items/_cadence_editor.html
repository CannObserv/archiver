{# Cadence editor — the announced fetch cadence (archiver#158). Posts to /dashboard/info-items/{id}/watch-cadence, which writes info_items.watch_spec and announces it on info.registry. hx-swap="none" like its sibling action forms: the section root carries hx-trigger="watcherUpdated from:body", and the response's HX-Trigger fires it, so the panel re-renders itself once. Swapping the response in *and* firing the trigger would render twice. Not gated on can_act — cadence is Archiver's own policy. Included from the `watching` AND `no_status` branches of _watcher_section.html: a freshly registered item sits in no_status until Watcher's first status frame, which is exactly when an operator wants to revise the cadence they just picked. `not_watching` offers "Begin Watching" instead and `degraded` shows an error, so neither includes this. The blank option is "delegate" — the consumer applies its own default, which may be a per-domain one rather than a global constant, and it is only reachable because the route replaces the document instead of merging into it. Vocabulary comes from src/dashboard/cadence.py via the route context, not a literal list here; the schema admits more intervals than the dropdown offers. #}
